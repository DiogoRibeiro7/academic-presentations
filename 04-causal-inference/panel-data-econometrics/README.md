# panel-data-econometrics

## Overview

This topic develops panel-data econometrics for causal analysis, focusing on fixed effects, random effects, unobserved heterogeneity, within-unit identification, clustered uncertainty, and the limitations of static panel estimators.

## Scope

- pooled OLS as a baseline;
- one-way and two-way fixed effects;
- within transformation;
- time-invariant unobserved heterogeneity;
- random effects and the exogeneity assumption;
- Hausman-style comparison logic;
- clustered and heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors;
- serial correlation and cross-sectional dependence;
- time-varying confounding and treatment timing;
- dynamic panels and Nickell bias;
- interpretation of within-unit causal effects.

## Design principle

Panel structure creates repeated observations, not automatic causal identification. The source of identifying variation and the exogeneity assumptions must be stated explicitly.
