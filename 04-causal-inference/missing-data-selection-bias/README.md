# missing-data-selection-bias

## Overview

This topic develops causal inference under missing outcomes, attrition, sample selection, and incomplete covariate information.

## Scope

- MCAR, MAR, and MNAR;
- outcome attrition;
- treatment-dependent missingness;
- complete-case analysis;
- inverse-probability-of-observation weighting;
- multiple imputation for causal analyses;
- selection diagrams and collider bias;
- Lee bounds;
- principal-stratification intuition;
- pattern-mixture and selection-model sensitivity;
- missing-not-at-random sensitivity;
- reporting and diagnostics.

## Design principle

Missingness is part of the causal design. A complete-case estimate is valid only under assumptions about why observations are missing and how that missingness relates to treatment, outcome, and covariates.
