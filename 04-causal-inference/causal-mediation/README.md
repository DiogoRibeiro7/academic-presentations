# causal-mediation

## Overview

This topic develops causal mediation analysis for decomposing total treatment effects into pathways operating through an intermediate variable and pathways operating outside that mediator.

## Scope

- total, direct, and indirect effects;
- mediator potential outcomes;
- natural direct and indirect effects;
- controlled direct effects;
- sequential ignorability;
- mediator-outcome confounding;
- post-treatment confounding;
- exposure-mediator interaction;
- interventional direct and indirect effects;
- sensitivity analysis;
- mechanism interpretation.

## Design principle

Mediation analysis is not ordinary regression decomposition. Direct and indirect causal effects require additional cross-world or interventional assumptions beyond those needed to identify the total treatment effect.
