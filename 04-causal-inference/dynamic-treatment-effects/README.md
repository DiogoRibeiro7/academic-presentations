# dynamic-treatment-effects

## Overview

This topic develops causal models in which treatment effects evolve over time, with emphasis on lags, carry-over, anticipation, cumulative effects, and dynamic identification in panel and time-series settings.

## Scope

- immediate and delayed treatment effects;
- distributed-lag models;
- cumulative treatment effects;
- event-time estimands;
- anticipation;
- carry-over and persistence;
- treatment-effect heterogeneity over time;
- dynamic confounding and feedback;
- lagged outcomes in panel models;
- treatment timing;
- impulse-response style summaries;
- long-run effects and finite-horizon interpretation.

## Design principle

A static treatment coefficient is often an average over a dynamic response process. Credible causal analysis should align the estimand with the temporal mechanism through which treatment acts.
