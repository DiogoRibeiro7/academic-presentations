# heterogeneous-treatment-effects

## Overview

This topic develops estimation of heterogeneous causal effects, with emphasis on conditional average treatment effects, honest subgroup discovery, causal trees and forests, orthogonalization, validation, and treatment targeting.

## Scope

- CATE and subgroup estimands;
- effect modification versus prognostic prediction;
- causal trees;
- causal forests;
- honest sample splitting;
- nuisance models and orthogonalization;
- overlap and extrapolation;
- calibration and validation of heterogeneity;
- policy targeting and uplift;
- multiple-testing and subgroup instability.

## Design principle

Treatment-effect heterogeneity is harder to estimate than outcomes because the individual causal effect is never observed. Flexible models need strong identification, honest validation, and restraint against overinterpreting noisy subgroups.
