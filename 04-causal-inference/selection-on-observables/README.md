# selection-on-observables

## Overview

This topic develops causal estimation under observed confounding, with emphasis on propensity scores, matching, weighting, balance diagnostics, outcome regression, and doubly robust estimators.

## Scope

- conditional exchangeability / ignorability;
- positivity and overlap;
- consistency;
- propensity-score estimation;
- matching and calipers;
- inverse-probability weighting;
- stabilized weights;
- covariate balance;
- effective sample size;
- outcome regression;
- augmented IPW;
- double robustness;
- trimming and estimand changes;
- unmeasured confounding and sensitivity.

## Design principle

Adjustment methods can remove bias from observed confounders only under a defensible treatment-assignment model. Balance after adjustment is necessary, but cannot prove that important unobserved confounding is absent.
