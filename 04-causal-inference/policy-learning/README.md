# policy-learning

## Overview

This topic develops policy learning and treatment targeting after causal-effect estimation, with emphasis on treatment rules, policy value, regret, off-policy evaluation, uplift, budget constraints, and decision risk.

## Scope

- individualized treatment rules;
- CATE-to-policy translation;
- treatment costs and thresholds;
- policy value and regret;
- off-policy evaluation;
- uplift and targeting curves;
- budget and capacity constraints;
- fairness and harm constraints;
- validation and deployment risk.

## Design principle

A good treatment-effect model is not automatically a good decision rule. Policy learning should optimize an explicit value function under costs, constraints, uncertainty, and the population where the rule will be deployed.
