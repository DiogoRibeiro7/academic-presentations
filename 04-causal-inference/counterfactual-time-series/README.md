# counterfactual-time-series

## Overview

This topic develops causal time-series designs for interventions observed over time, with emphasis on interrupted time series and synthetic control.

## Scope

- intervention timing and counterfactual trajectories;
- segmented regression;
- level and slope changes;
- autocorrelation and seasonality;
- structural breaks and concurrent shocks;
- control-series extensions;
- synthetic control donor pools;
- convex weights and pre-treatment fit;
- placebo units and placebo intervention dates;
- RMSPE-style fit diagnostics;
- spillovers and donor contamination;
- uncertainty and sensitivity.

## Design principle

A time-series break is not causal merely because it occurs after treatment. Credibility depends on whether the untreated trajectory can be defended from temporal structure, comparison series, donor units, or a combination of these.
