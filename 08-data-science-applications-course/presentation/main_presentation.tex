\begin{frame}{Integrated Synthesis}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Production data science is a coupled statistical-and-systems problem: model error, data quality, serving behavior, monitoring, and decision rules interact.
    \item More complex models are justified only when they improve the target objective under the actual latency, reliability, governance, and maintenance constraints.
    \item Explanations are evidence about model behavior under a chosen method; they do not automatically establish causality, fairness, or model adequacy.
    \item Time-dependent systems require evaluation at realistic forecast origins or decision times, with explicit treatment of drift, delayed labels, and changing information sets.
    \item Deployment decisions should combine predictive evidence, uncertainty, operational failure modes, rollback/recovery paths, and domain-specific consequences.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
